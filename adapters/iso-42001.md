# ISO/IEC 42001:2023

**Version:** 2023
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-22
**Official Reference:** https://www.iso.org/standard/42001

Mapping of SEAT outcomes to ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system clauses, centered on the competence, awareness, leadership, and performance-evaluation requirements that a security awareness program provides audit evidence for.

The first certifiable AI management-system standard, built on the same Plan-Do-Check-Act clause structure as ISO 27001 (clauses 4-10) plus an Annex A of AI-specific controls. Primary clauses: 5.1 leadership and commitment, 5.2 AI policy, 6.1 AI risk assessment and treatment, 7.2 competence, 7.3 awareness, 7.4 communication, 9.1 monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation, 9.3 management review, 10.1 continual improvement, 10.2 nonconformity and corrective action. Annex A controls are referenced at the control-group level (e.g. A.3 internal organization, A.9 responsible use) rather than sub-control numbers. Certification is the teeth: an auditor looks for objective evidence that these clauses are operating, and SEAT maturity data is exactly that evidence for the workforce side of the AIMS.

## How an assistant applies this adapter

Given per-question maturity scores (1-5), compute outcome scores by averaging the scores of all questions that map to each SEAT outcome (see outcomes.md for the mapping). Then for each requirement below:

- **Met:** outcome score >= threshold for that requirement's assurance level
- **Partial:** outcome score > 0 but below threshold
- **Gap:** outcome score is 0 or no questions answered for that outcome

Thresholds by assurance level:
- Required: >= 4.5
- Expected: >= 2.5
- Recommended: >= 1.5

For gaps and partial results on Required or Expected requirements, list the missing evidence types.

## Outcome-to-Requirement Mapping

| SEAT Outcome | Framework Reference | Assurance | Evidence Required | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| strategic-alignment | Clause 4.1, 4.2, 5.2 | Required | Policy Document, Assessment Report | 5 | Clause 5.2 requires an AI policy aligned to the organization context (4.1) and interested-party needs (4.2). A program strategy that ties workforce AI literacy to the organization's actual AI use cases is what makes that policy operational rather than a framed statement on the wall. Cadence: Annual. |
| governance-documentation | Clause 5.2, Annex A.2, Annex A.3 | Required | Policy Document, Assessment Report | 4 | Annex A.2 (policies related to AI) and A.3 (internal organization, roles and responsibilities) require documented ownership. If nobody can produce the page naming who owns AI acceptable use and workforce training, the AIMS fails the documentation test an auditor applies first. Cadence: Annual. |
| executive-support | Clause 5.1 | Required | Board Minutes, Policy Document | 5 | Clause 5.1 makes top management demonstrate leadership and commitment to the AIMS, including resourcing it. Evidence that leadership reviewed AI risk and funded the program is a direct certification artifact, not a nice-to-have. Cadence: Semi-Annual. |
| continuous-improvement | Clause 9.3, 10.1 | Required | Assessment Report, Incident Report | 4 | Clause 10.1 requires continual improvement of the AIMS and 9.3 requires management review to drive it. A program that revises its AI guidance on a cadence and after incidents produces the improvement record surveillance audits look for. Cadence: Quarterly. |
| targeted-communication | Clause 7.3, 7.4 | Required | Communication Artifact, Training Record, Curriculum Document | 4 | Clause 7.4 requires determining internal communications relevant to the AIMS and 7.3 requires awareness of the AI policy and each person's contribution. Role-targeted communication is how both are met for a workforce rather than a single all-staff memo. Cadence: Continuous. |
| cultural-relevance | Clause 7.3 | Recommended | Communication Artifact, Feedback Survey | 2 | Clause 7.3 awareness is only real when people understand why the AI policy matters to their work. Messaging that resonates across roles is what turns required awareness into a culture that actually questions AI output. Cadence: Continuous. |
| active-participation | Clause 10.2, Annex A.9 | Expected | Incident Report, Feedback Survey | 4 | Annex A.9 (responsible use of AI systems) and clause 10.2 (nonconformity and corrective action) depend on people surfacing problems. A workforce that reports misuse or unexpected AI behavior feeds the nonconformity process the standard requires. Cadence: Continuous. |
| feedback-responsiveness | Clause 10.2 | Expected | Feedback Survey, Incident Report | 3 | Clause 10.2 requires reacting to nonconformities and correcting them. If staff report AI problems and nothing changes, the corrective-action loop is not operating and the finding writes itself. Cadence: As Needed. |
| performance-measurement | Clause 9.1 | Required | Metric Report, Assessment Report | 5 | Clause 9.1 requires the organization to determine what to monitor and measure about the AIMS and to evaluate its performance and effectiveness. SEAT maturity measurement over time is the effectiveness evidence for the workforce controls, which is what a certification auditor asks to see. Cadence: Continuous. |
| risk-driven-assessment | Clause 6.1, Annex A.5 | Required | Assessment Report, Policy Document | 5 | Clause 6.1 requires an AI risk assessment and treatment process, and Annex A.5 requires AI system impact assessments. Program effort should follow that risk picture, concentrating on the AI uses with the highest impact rather than treating every use the same. Cadence: Semi-Annual. |
| relevant-tailored-training | Clause 7.2 | Required | Curriculum Document, Training Record, Assessment Report | 5 | Clause 7.2 requires that people doing work affecting AIMS performance are competent and that the organization retains evidence of competence. Role-appropriate training is the mechanism, and training records are the retained evidence. Cadence: Annual. |
| effective-learning-methods | Clause 7.2 | Expected | Training Record, Simulation Results, Feedback Survey | 4 | Clause 7.2 asks the organization to take actions to acquire competence and to evaluate the effectiveness of those actions. Methods that demonstrably change behavior, not just record attendance, are what satisfy the effectiveness half of that clause. Cadence: As Needed. |
| accessible-inclusive-training | Clause 7.3 | Expected | Communication Artifact | 3 | Clause 7.3 awareness applies to persons doing work under the organization's control, which includes contractors and staff outside the AI team. Training that reaches everyone in scope is what keeps the awareness control from having gaps an auditor can sample into. Cadence: Continuous. |
| integrated-training-lifecycle | Clause 7.2, Annex A.4 | Expected | Training Record | 4 | Annex A.4 (resources for AI systems, including competence) and clause 7.2 expect competence to be maintained across the lifecycle. Embedding AI competence from onboarding and refreshing it on role change keeps the control current rather than a one-time event. Cadence: Onboarding. |
